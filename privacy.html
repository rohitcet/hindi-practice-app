Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Hindi Practice PSLE ("the app") is a Hindi-language PSLE exam practice tool. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who to contact with questions.

Information we collect

  • Google account info — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture via Supabase Auth, our authentication provider. We use this only to identify your account and track your practice progress.
  • Payment information — if you choose to unlock lifetime access, payment is processed entirely by Stripe. We never see, receive, or store your card details.
  • Practice activity — quiz scores and session history are stored so you (and, for admin accounts, a parent/teacher) can track progress over time.

What we don't do

  • The Android app shows no advertisements and includes no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Oral practice recordings are stored only on your own device — sending a recording for expert feedback is optional and happens via an email you send yourself.

Microphone access

The Oral Practice feature requests microphone access solely to record your spoken answers for your own review and optional submission for feedback. Recordings are not uploaded to our servers automatically.

Children's privacy

This app is designed for PSLE-level students (typically ages 11-12). We collect only the minimum information described above (via a parent/guardian-managed Google account) and do not knowingly collect any additional personal information from children.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
